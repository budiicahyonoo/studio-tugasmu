
# Studio Tugasmu — Design Direction

## Identity

Studio Tugasmu is a modern academic service platform that helps students access academic assistance with transparent pricing, clear service information, and a convenient ordering experience.

The visual identity should communicate:

- Professional and trustworthy
- Modern and approachable
- Clean and organized
- Friendly without looking childish
- Premium without feeling overly corporate

## Color Palette

**Primary — Deep Navy**

- Hex: `#16324F`
- Used for primary buttons, headings, navigation emphasis, and important UI elements.

**Secondary — Teal**

- Hex: `#167D8D`
- Used for secondary actions, links, interactive states, and selected UI accents.

**Accent — Soft Amber**

- Hex: `#F4B860`
- Used sparingly for highlights, badges, small indicators, and important supporting details.

**Background — Cool White**

- Hex: `#F7F8FA`
- Main page background and subtle section separation.

**Surface — White**

- Hex: `#FFFFFF`
- Cards, forms, dialogs, and content surfaces.

**Typography Colors**

- Primary Text: `#172B40`
- Secondary Text: `#64748B`
- Border: `#DCE4EB`

## Color Usage Rules

- Deep Navy must establish the primary visual identity.
- Teal acts as a supporting color, not a competing primary color.
- Amber must remain an accent and should never dominate large sections.
- White and Cool White should make up most of the interface.
- Maintain sufficient contrast for readability and accessibility.
- Avoid excessive colored backgrounds and unnecessary color combinations.

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans throughout the website.

Typography should feel modern, readable, and professional.

Establish a consistent hierarchy between:

- Page headings
- Section headings
- Body text
- Supporting descriptions
- Labels and metadata

Avoid excessively small text and inconsistent font weights.

## Visual Style

- Clean, modern, and structured interface.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.
- Subtle borders and restrained shadows.
- Rounded corners used consistently.
- Professional card and form layouts.
- Strong emphasis on pricing transparency and service clarity.

## Components

Maintain consistent styling across:

- Navigation and footer
- Buttons and links
- Service category cards
- Pricing and simulation forms
- Input fields and dropdowns
- Portfolio sections
- Customer reviews
- Dialogs and notifications
- Loading, empty, error, and success states

## Layout & UX

Prioritize user experience and information clarity.

The pricing simulation must be easy to understand and use.

Service categories should be visually distinguishable without introducing unnecessary decoration.

All sections should feel like parts of one cohesive product.

## Dials

ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

Use a balanced and confident visual presentation, moderate visual contrast, and subtle interaction feedback.

## Avoid

- Generic SaaS template aesthetics.
- Excessive gradients.
- Random decorative elements.
- Too many competing accent colors.
- Inconsistent spacing and border radius.
- Unnecessary animations.
- Excessive card-based layouts.
- Low-contrast text.
- Inconsistent component styling.

The final result should feel like a thoughtfully designed academic service platform, not a generic website template.
